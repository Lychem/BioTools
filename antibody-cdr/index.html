ℹ️ Accuracy note: This tool uses Smith-Waterman alignment against canonical germline references (IGHV1-2, IGHV3-23, IGHV4-34, IGKV1-39, IGKV3-20, IGLV2-14) for numbering. It works well for typical germline-like antibody sequences. For highly mutated, synthetic, or nanobody sequences, consider ANARCI (HMM-based). CDR3 boundaries are detected by the conserved FR4 tryptophan/phenylalanine motif.
